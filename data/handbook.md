# Engineering Handbook

## Deployments
Production deployments require approval from the platform team. Create a change record before requesting approval.

Deployments run through the continuous delivery pipeline. Never deploy directly from a developer laptop.

## Accounts
Use the Forgot Password link on the sign-in page to reset an account password. The reset link expires after 30 minutes.

Enable multi-factor authentication for every production account. Store recovery codes in the approved password manager.

## Incidents
Declare a production incident in the incident channel and assign an incident commander. The commander coordinates communication and recovery.

Every production incident needs a blameless postmortem within five business days.
